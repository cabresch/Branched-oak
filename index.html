About the depth map

Depth contours come from the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission lake bathymetry program (Lake Contours layer on NebraskaMap), surveyed with the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation. Branched Oak has ten contours at 3 foot intervals, 3 ft to 30 ft.

Depths are referenced to the survey pool. In the data, the 3 ft contour sits at elevation 1281.0 ft, which puts the survey water surface at about 1284 ft. If the lake is below that level, actual depth is less than the contour shows.

Buen Camino draws 2.5 ft (wing keel). The 3 ft line is drawn in red: inside it you have about 6 inches under the keel at survey pool, and less if the lake is down. The 6 ft line is orange.

Contours load from the Game and Parks service when online and are cached on this phone so the map still works with weak signal. Basemap tiles need signal unless recently viewed.

Crew sign in

Sign in once per phone. Marks and sails then sync to the shared list, so both of you see the same things and nothing is lost if the app is reinstalled.

GPS and screen

Recording a track asks the phone to keep the screen on (a wake lock). Tracking pauses if you switch apps or lock the phone; it resumes when you come back. Keep the phone mounted with this page open while underway.

Add this page to your home screen for a full-screen view: Safari share menu, then Add to Home Screen.
